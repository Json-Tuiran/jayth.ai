It does the work, not just explains it

An AI chat tells you what to do. Jayth does it on your computer, in plain sight, and asks your permission for what matters.

Codes and checks

It writes the code, runs it, reads the errors and fixes them. For projects there's Jayth Code: explorer, editor and terminal.

Uses your apps

It reads the buttons and fields of any app, clicks, types and manages windows. Moving the mouse to a corner stops it.

Browses in plain sight

Its built-in browser searches, clicks and fills in forms with its own pointer. You can step in at any time.

Documents and canvas

It creates Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files. On the canvas you see web pages and documents, edit them or ask for changes, and export them.

No cloud, no GPU needed

Once the model is downloaded it works offline. It runs on a laptop without a graphics card, and uses NVIDIA, AMD, Intel Arc or Apple Silicon if you have them.

Any model you like

Download GGUF models from the app (it marks the ones that fit), use your own, Ollama or LM Studio, or an API model such as Claude or Gemini.

You're in charge

Approve every action or give it free rein; risky actions always ask. If a website tries to give it orders, it warns you. And you can undo its changes.

Skills and services

Teach it procedures with SKILL.md skills (also as .zip), create assistants, and connect Gmail, Drive, Notion and more with your own account.

In your language

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese and Chinese. It answers in the language you write in.

What computer do I need?

Jayth runs on almost any computer, but how fast and how smoothly the agent works depends on your hardware: the AI model reads what you give it and writes its answer on your processor or graphics card. More power means faster answers and more capable models; with less, it still works, just more slowly and with small models.

ReadingBefore answering, the model reads your message, its instructions and your files. This is where a graphics card helps most.
WritingThe answer comes out word by word. It depends mostly on memory speed and the size of the model.
Thinking betterBigger models make better agents, but they need more memory. The setup assistant tells you which ones fit.

It works, with some patience

  • 4-core processor (2018 or newer).
  • 8 GB of RAM (16 GB recommended).
  • No graphics card.
  • About 10 GB of free disk space.
  • Small models, like Gemma 4 E4B (about 4 GB).
Rough speedSlow

Measured on an i5-8250U with 20 GB and no GPU: it reads about 22 tokens/s and writes about 5 (3–4 words per second). Fine for short, focused tasks; the first answer in each conversation can take a few minutes, and long tasks quite a bit more.

Smooth for everyday work

  • 16 GB of RAM or more.
  • A graphics card with 8 GB (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel Arc), or a Mac with an Apple chip and 16 GB.
  • Medium models, around 8 to 14 billion parameters.
Rough speedGood

Several times faster than without a GPU, with more capable models. Integrated graphics usually don't help: Jayth then uses the processor.

Big models, no waiting

  • A graphics card with 16–24 GB, or a Mac with an Apple chip and 32 GB or more.
  • Big models, 27 to 32 billion parameters (like Qwen3.8 27B).
Rough speedVery good

Big models make the best agents. They also run without a GPU, but very slowly: Qwen3.8 27B writes under 1 token/s on the test laptop.

Use an API model

  • Any computer that runs Jayth.
  • A key for Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter or another compatible service.
Rough speedFast (depends on the service)

Fast even on a modest computer, and you can switch back to a local model whenever you like. In exchange, you pay the service for what you use and your messages go through it.

On first launch, the setup assistant checks your computer and shows which models fit and their approximate speed. And Optimize for this computer (Settings › Performance) measures yours and picks the fastest settings.

Your data stays with you

An agent that uses your computer has to be trustworthy. That's why its code is public and the important things always need your OK.

  • No accounts, no trackingNo sign-up and no usage statistics. This website uses no cookies or analytics either.
  • Everything on your computerWith a local model, your conversations and files never leave your computer.
  • Risky actions always askDeleting, installing software, or posting and sending from your accounts needs your OK, even in automatic mode.
  • Guards against web tricksIf a page tries to give it orders, it warns you and asks before anything that changes something.
  • Protected keysAPI keys are kept in a vault: encrypted on Windows, readable only by your user on macOS and Linux.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. Jayth is free and open source (GPL-3.0 license), with no ads and no accounts, for companies too. Local models are free as well. If you choose an API model such as Claude or Gemini, that service charges you for what you use.

Why is it slow on my computer?

Because the model runs on your processor or graphics card: without a GPU, or with a model that's big for your computer, it reads and writes more slowly, and the first answer in each conversation takes longer because it reads its instructions. Try a smaller model, click Optimize for this computer in Settings › Performance, or use an API model for tasks that need speed. More in What computer do I need.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the app and the model the first time. After that it works offline; web searches and connected services are only used when you're online.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

It shows up because the installers aren't signed yet: free signing for open-source projects is in progress. Click More info → Run anyway. To check that your download is the original, compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one in that release's SHA256SUMS.txt.

How is it different from Ollama or LM Studio?

Ollama and LM Studio let you chat with local models. Jayth also takes action: it creates and edits files, runs commands, and uses apps and the web, always with your approval. If you already use Ollama or LM Studio, Jayth can use them as its engine.

Can it break something on my computer?

You choose how much freedom it gets, from read-only to free rein. Deleting, installing software or acting on your accounts always asks. Deleted files go to the recycle bin, and you can undo the file changes it makes.

How can I help?

Use it and tell us what breaks or what you miss (from the app: profile menu › Send feedback…), star it on GitHub or recommend it. More ways on Support Jayth.
